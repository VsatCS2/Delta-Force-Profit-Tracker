Home / In-game overlays

Check in without leaving the game

Two small overlays, each with its own hotkey. Both are off until you turn them on in Settings.

Profit overlay. Press Ctrl+Shift+D to show your profit or loss for today, or for your current session, along with your best raid and your most recent loot. You can't click it, so it never gets in your way, and the same hotkey hides it.

Player search overlay. Press Ctrl+Shift+O, type a name, and see that player's K/D overall and on easy, normal and hard, their extraction rate, current rank, and net stash. Results are cached, so searching the same name again is instant. It uses your free DF Tracker account: sign in with Discord in Settings first.

You can change either hotkey. The overlays are Windows only, and like any pop-up window they can't show over exclusive fullscreen, so use windowed or borderless mode. The same lookup is also a full page in the app, with accuracy, knocks, scores and stash value as well.
