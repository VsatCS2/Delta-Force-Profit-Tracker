What it does

Everything runs in one window, with a small overlay you can pull up during a match.

  • Profit per match

    Every match is logged with the credits it earned. See your wins and losses, win rate, and average profit per match at a glance, and refresh today's matches with one click.

  • Best maps and operators

    Find out which map and which operator make you the most credits, then open either one to see the matches behind the number.

  • Match history and weekly report

    Browse your past matches, sync the ones you missed, and check a weekly report of how your profit is trending.

  • Player lookup

    Type any name to see that player's K/D, extraction rate, accuracy, headshot rate, and stash value. It works for teammates, opponents, or yourself.

  • In-game overlay

    Press Ctrl+Shift+O during a match to search a name in a small window on top of the game, then dismiss it and keep playing.

  • Price alerts

    Track market prices for high-value items and set alerts, so you know what your loot is worth before you sell.

  • Community leaderboard

    Compare your profit with other players on the community leaderboard. Joining is optional.

Also included: several color themes and automatic updates.

Know who you're up against

Player lookup gives you a player's combat record and wealth in seconds. Results are cached, so searching the same name again is instant.

Your stash value is shown as liquid, fixed, and collection assets, so you can tell a veteran from a fresh account before the match starts.

Questions

The short answers.

Does DF Tracker touch the game?

No. It runs as a separate app. It doesn't read the game's memory, inject code, or automate anything in the game, and the overlay is its own window. Your own match data comes from the official Delta Force stats service, while player lookup uses a separate third-party data source.

Do I need to sign in?

Yes, to pull your own matches you sign in with your Delta Force (Level Infinite) account. Your password goes only to Level Infinite's login, never to DF Tracker's servers.

Where does my data go?

Your match history is stored on your own PC. Joining the community leaderboard is optional.

Which versions of Windows are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Is DF Tracker official?

No. It's an independent fan-made tool and isn't affiliated with Level Infinite, Proxima Beta, or the Delta Force developers.
