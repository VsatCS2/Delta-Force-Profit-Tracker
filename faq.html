Questions

The short answers.

Does DF Tracker touch the game?

No. It runs as a separate app. It doesn't read the game's memory, inject code, or automate anything in the game, and the overlays are their own windows. Your own match data comes from the official Delta Force stats service. Player lookups and item prices come from separate third-party data sources.

Is DF Tracker free?

Yes. Tracking your matches, the overlays, price alerts, door codes, the reds collection and the fishing tracker are all free to use.

Do I need to sign in?

There are two separate sign-ins, and only the first is needed to track your own matches. Your game account: sign in with your Delta Force (Level Infinite) account so DF Tracker can pull your matches. Your password goes only to Level Infinite's login, never to DF Tracker's servers. A DF Tracker account (optional): sign in with Discord to use player lookup and the player search overlay.

What is a DF Tracker account?

It's a free account you create by signing in with Discord. It lets our server know who is looking up players, so lookups can be limited fairly per person. It is separate from your game account, and DF Tracker only uses Discord to confirm who you are.

What do you store about me?

For a DF Tracker account: your Discord ID, email address, display name and avatar link as Discord provides them, your plan, how many lookups you've used each day, and when you last used the app. If you join the community leaderboard: your Overview totals and recent matches under your in-game nickname. Your own match history stays on your PC. The privacy page has the full details.

How do the overlays work?

Both overlays are off until you turn them on in Settings. The profit overlay opens with Ctrl+Shift+D and the player search overlay with Ctrl+Shift+O, and you can change either hotkey. The search overlay needs your DF Tracker account. Like any pop-up window, the overlays can't show over exclusive fullscreen, so play in windowed or borderless mode.

Are price alerts for buying or selling?

Buying. You pick an item and the price you'd like to pay, and DF Tracker notifies you when the item's auction price drops to that price or lower.

Where do the door codes come from?

The app asks the game's own web service for the current private room door codes for each map when you open the Door Codes page. They change, so the page refreshes them for you and you can press Refresh any time.

What are the reds worth on the Reds page?

Each item shows the game's default quick-sell price per item, and the stack total is that price times how many you hold. That isn't always what the item sells for at auction.

What does the website's Try it page do?

It looks up one player's public in-game stats so you can see what player lookup shows. Each visitor gets 3 free lookups a day to keep the service fair. The full app shows much more and isn't limited the same way.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Contact us from the privacy page and we'll delete your DF Tracker account and the data attached to it. Leaving the community leaderboard also removes your stats from it.

Which versions of Windows are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Why does Windows warn me when I run the installer?

DF Tracker isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen can show a "Windows protected your PC" notice for a new download. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Every release lists a SHA-256 checksum so you can confirm your download is the file we published.

Is DF Tracker official?

No. It's an independent fan-made tool and isn't affiliated with Level Infinite, Proxima Beta, or the Delta Force developers.
