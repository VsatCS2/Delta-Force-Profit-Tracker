# Delta Force Profit Tracker v2.1.0

## What's new

**Door Codes.** A new **Door Codes** page shows the private room door code for every map. Click a code to copy it. It doesn't need a login.

**Reds.** A new **Reds** page lists every red item you have collected, with its name and picture, how many you have, and what they are worth. The top of the page shows your total reds, how many different reds you have, and their combined value. You can search by name and sort by value, count or name. This page needs you to be logged in.

**Fishing.** A new **Fishing** page shows your fishing level, how many fish you have caught, your catch rate, best streak and days fished, and every kind of fish you have caught, with its picture, grade, how many you caught and your longest and heaviest one. You can search by name and sort by number caught, grade, length or name. Double-click a fish to open its details: your heaviest, lightest, longest and shortest catch with the date of each, where it can be caught, its recommended bait, the ecology notes you have unlocked (the rest show how to unlock them), and your record for it (best quality, total catches and your first catch). This page needs you to be logged in.

## Updating

- **On 2.0.2 or earlier:** open **Settings -> Check for Updates** and let the app install it.
- **New to DF Tracker:** download `DeltaForceTracker-Setup-2.1.0.exe`.
- `DeltaForceTracker.zip` is the same program as a plain folder (it's also what the in-app updater downloads). Extract the whole folder, not just the `.exe`.
