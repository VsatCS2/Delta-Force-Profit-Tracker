Home / Reds collection

Every red you've found, with counts and values

The Reds page lists each red item you've collected with its name and picture, how many you hold, and what it's worth.

What you get

  • Names and pictures instead of item numbers
  • The price of each item and the total for the stack
  • Totals at the top: unique reds, how many you hold, and their combined value
  • Search, and sort by value, count or name

Prices are the game's default quick-sell price. The auction house price can differ.
