Track what every Delta Force match actually pays.

DF Tracker is a Windows app that adds up your credits for every match and shows where your profit really comes from, by map and by operator. Press a hotkey to see your profit or look up a player without leaving the game, and get an alert when an item you want drops to your price.

Your profit, in detail

Everything DF Tracker records about your matches, in one window.

  • Overview

    See what you've made today, this week, this month, and all time, with daily, weekly and monthly breakdowns. Your best raid and your most recent high-value items sit right below.

  • Play sessions

    Start a session to track a block of play on its own, separate from calendar days, so a late night isn't split between yesterday and today. When you're done, copy a short summary of your net, matches, win rate and best raid to the clipboard.

  • Weekly highlights

    Your evac rate and K/D for the week, your best and worst squad-mates by the value you extracted or lost together, your 7-day net worth, your most valuable items, and your best raid of the week, with a 7-day trend chart.

  • Maps and operators

    Every map with its matches, wins and losses, and net income. Every operator with the same, plus win rate and average profit per match, so you can see exactly how much each operator has earned you. Sort any column, and double-click an operator to see its matches.

  • Match history

    Browse every match with its result, kills, operator, rank and net income. Search and sort, then open any match for the squad breakdown, kills against operators and bots, and what you carried out. Refresh today's matches in one click, or sync to catch older ones.

  • Profile and extended stats

    Your game profile, plus extended stats like accuracy, knocks, kills and deaths by difficulty, category scores, and stash value.

Check in without leaving the game

Two small overlays, each with its own hotkey. Both are off until you turn them on in Settings.

Profit overlay. Press Ctrl+Shift+D to show your profit or loss for today, or for your current session, along with your best raid and your most recent loot. You can't click it, so it never gets in your way, and the same hotkey hides it.

Player search overlay. Press Ctrl+Shift+O, type a name, and see that player's K/D overall and on easy, normal and hard, their extraction rate, current rank, and net stash. Results are cached, so searching the same name again is instant. It uses your free DF Tracker account: sign in with Discord in Settings first.

You can change either hotkey. The overlays are Windows only, and like any pop-up window they can't show over exclusive fullscreen, so use windowed or borderless mode. The same lookup is also a full page in the app, with accuracy, knocks, scores and stash value as well.

Know when an item hits the price you want to pay

Pick an item, set the price you'd buy it at, and DF Tracker notifies you when its auction price drops to that price or lower. You can watch several items at once.

It checks every minute while the app is open, even when it's minimized or in the system tray. You get a pop-up and, if you like, a sound. Enter prices as 5,000,000, 5m, or 750k.

Prices can trail the live auction house slightly. Each alert shows whether its price has been met or it's still waiting.

Questions

The short answers.

Does DF Tracker touch the game?

No. It runs as a separate app. It doesn't read the game's memory, inject code, or automate anything in the game, and the overlays are their own windows. Your own match data comes from the official Delta Force stats service. Player lookups and item prices come from separate third-party data sources.

Do I need to sign in?

There are two separate sign-ins, and only the first is needed to track your own matches. Your game account: sign in with your Delta Force (Level Infinite) account so DF Tracker can pull your matches. Your password goes only to Level Infinite's login, never to DF Tracker's servers. A DF Tracker account (optional): sign in with Discord to use player lookup and the player search overlay.

What is a DF Tracker account?

It's a free account you create by signing in with Discord. It lets our server know who is looking up players, so lookups can be limited fairly per person. It is separate from your game account, and DF Tracker only uses Discord to confirm who you are.

What do you store about me?

For a DF Tracker account: your Discord ID, email address, display name and avatar link as Discord provides them, your plan, how many lookups you've used each day, and when you last used the app. If you join the community leaderboard: your Overview totals and recent matches under your in-game nickname. Your own match history stays on your PC. The privacy page has the full details.

How do the overlays work?

Both overlays are off until you turn them on in Settings. The profit overlay opens with Ctrl+Shift+D and the player search overlay with Ctrl+Shift+O, and you can change either hotkey. The search overlay needs your DF Tracker account. Like any pop-up window, the overlays can't show over exclusive fullscreen, so play in windowed or borderless mode.

Are price alerts for buying or selling?

Buying. You pick an item and the price you'd like to pay, and DF Tracker notifies you when the item's auction price drops to that price or lower.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Contact us from the privacy page and we'll delete your DF Tracker account and the data attached to it. Leaving the community leaderboard also removes your stats from it.

Which versions of Windows are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Is DF Tracker official?

No. It's an independent fan-made tool and isn't affiliated with Level Infinite, Proxima Beta, or the Delta Force developers.
